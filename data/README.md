# Data

`data/processed/` is generated by `python -m pipeline all` and committed, so the app works right after a clone.
`data/raw/` holds the downloads and is git-ignored (about 140 MB; re-fetched automatically).

| File | Contents | Derived from (licence) |
|---|---|---|
| `districts.json` | 30 districts: names and spellings used by each source, centroid, coastal / KBK flags | geoBoundaries (ODbL 1.0) |
| `districts.geojson` | Simplified district boundaries for the map | geoBoundaries gbOpen IND ADM2 2021 (ODbL 1.0) |
| `yields_odisha.csv` | Area, production and yield for every crop and season, 1997-98 onwards | DES + UPAg via India Data Portal (ODC-By) |
| `kharif_rice.csv` | The forecast target: Kharif (autumn + winter) rice per district-season, with quality labels | DES + UPAg (ODC-By) |
| `weather_monthly.csv` | Monthly rainfall and temperature per district with 1991-2020 normals | NASA POWER |
| `backtest.csv` | Out-of-sample forecasts of the deployed system for every past season and issue date | derived |
| `forecasts.json` | Current-season forecasts, intervals and drivers, plus state-wide figures | derived |
| `evaluation.json` | Every candidate's scores, baselines, coverage and the protocol | derived |

Database rights: the boundary-derived files are a derivative database under the

Statistics / UPAg, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, via India Data Portal".

Notes on the statistics:

- Yields are **milled-rice** equivalent, as DES publishes them (roughly 0.67 x paddy).
- On the years both cover (2013-14 to 2022-23), UPAg's "Kharif" equals DES's "Autumn", UPAg's "Rabi" equals DES's
  "Winter" and "Summer" is unchanged; from 2023-24 UPAg reports one combined Kharif figure. The pipeline uses DES
  wherever it exists.
- No value is removed. District-seasons that look unusual compared with their own history and their five nearest
  districts are labelled in `qc_flag` (`unusually_low` / `unusually_high`).
