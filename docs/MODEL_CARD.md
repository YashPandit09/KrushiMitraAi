## Task

- **Target:** Kharif (autumn + winter) rice yield, tonnes per hectare (milled-rice basis, as published by DES).
- **Units of analysis:** 30 districts of Odisha, Kharif seasons 1998-2024 (810 district-seasons).
- **Issue dates:** after the end of July, August, September, October and November of the season.
- **Weather through:** 2026-10-21.

## Method

Forecast = district trend x (1 + weather adjustment).

- Trend: robust (Huber) linear fit per district on all earlier seasons.
- Adjustment: rainfall vs the 1991-2020 normal (season and last two months), root-zone soil moisture, daytime temperature, longest dry spell, heavy-rain days, wettest 3-day spell, cyclone wind and distance-weighted exposure (IBTrACS), coastal and KBK flags - pooled across districts.
- Candidates in the deployed pool: `trend|none|w=all`, `ridge|compact|w=all`, `lgbm|compact|w=all`, `ensemble|compact|w=all`.
- Deployment: For each lead time and season, the pool member with the lowest mean squared error over the previous 8 seasons is used.
- Uncertainty: split conformal on the previous 10 seasons' relative errors of the deployed system, same lead time.

## Evaluation protocol

- rolling-origin: the forecast for season t is trained only on seasons before t.
- Backtests start in 2003; validation seasons 2006-2014, test seasons 2015-2024.
- **Disclosure:** The first protocol picked a single model on a fixed validation period (2006-2014). On the test period (2015-2024) that choice lost to the plain trend. The deployed system therefore uses online selection: for each lead time and season it uses whichever pool member had the lowest error over the previous eight seasons - something a forecaster could have done in real time. Because this change was made after seeing the first test results, the test-period numbers of the online system are not fully independent; both protocols are reported side by side.

## Data quality

No official observation is removed. 46 district-seasons are labelled as unusual relative to their own history and their five nearest districts (most are real shocks: the 1999 Super Cyclone, the 2000/2002/2015 droughts, Cyclone Phailin in 2013, the 2017 brown planthopper outbreak). A sensitivity analysis without them is in `evaluation.json` (`sensitivity_excluding_flagged_rows`).

## Limitations

- Weather is sampled at one point per district from a ~50 km reanalysis grid (NASA POWER).
- No irrigation, variety, sowing-date or input data; much of the year-to-year variation is unexplained.
- Official district yields are estimates with reporting noise; intervals are therefore wide.
- Yields are milled-rice equivalent (DES convention). Not farm-level or financial advice.
- The online-selection protocol was adopted after seeing the first test results; seasons from 2025 on are the first fully independent evaluation.
